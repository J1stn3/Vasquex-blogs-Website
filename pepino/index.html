About JAMSPARTY GRAPHIC STUDIO CORP.

A leading creative advertising, commercial graphic design, and print production agency based in the creative hub of Bo. Obrero, Davao City, celebrated for over 22 years of design excellence.

Creative Excellence & Quality Print Craftsmanship

JAMSPARTY GRAPHIC STUDIO CORP. is a premier creative design and commercial print production hub in Davao City. For over 22 years, the company has catered to diverse corporate clients, local businesses, educational institutions, and food service brands across the region.

The studio specializes in end-to-end visual branding, commercial food menu design, product packaging labels, custom sticker plotting, Sintra board mounting, illuminated backlit signages, and large-format tarpaulin printing. Interns receive immersive, hands-on mentorship directly from industry practitioners, mastering both digital design software and physical production craft.

Learning Synthesis

Reflection & Key Learnings

What eight weeks at JAMSPARTY GRAPHIC STUDIO CORP. taught me about graphic design, prepress discipline, physical craftsmanship, and client communication.

01

Prepress Precision & Bleed Margins

A beautiful on-screen graphic means nothing if it is not print-ready. Working in CorelDRAW taught me the vital importance of color modes (CMYK), bleed tolerances, safe margins, and resolution standards before sending jobs to production.

02

Pride in Physical Craftsmanship

From manually trimming thousands of business cards and sticker labels to mounting vinyl onto Sintra boards and assembling backlit signages, I learned that graphic design is a physical craft that requires meticulous patience and attention to detail.

03

Client Dialogue & Team Collaboration

Joining Sir Eric on client site visits showed me how technical expertise translates into client trust. Translating client ideas into functional signage and working harmoniously with fellow trainees made this internship truly unforgettable.
